**MONITORING/FOLLOW-UP RULE (Traditionalist framing)**

Extra monitoring is acceptable if:
* It's **protocolized and predictable** (clear schedule, clear action thresholds)

It's a deal-breaker if:
* The monitoring is **complex, non-standard, or hard to execute reliably**
* It threatens **treatment consistency** or causes frequent interruptions
* There's an **established alternative** with a more stable, predictable protocol

I want **predictability**: clear monitoring schedule, clear action thresholds, and a pathway the MDT is comfortable executing consistently.
